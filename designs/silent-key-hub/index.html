    Changed from the render

    1. 8 stations, edge-on keys. The render shows 10 tabs from the top and keys facing outward. Eight 24 mm heads facing outward need ~200 mm of rim; the hub has ~170. Keys now hang edge-on in radial slots.
    2. Printed leaf spring instead of a coil spring. A spring and plunger that small won't print and is fiddly to buy. Each station's coloured top tab is now the tip of a flexure in the hub roof, pressing on a square cam.
    3. Real hard stops. 0°: fork meets the slot's back wall. 90°: fork meets the hood over the slot mouth. The detent holds, the stop takes the load when you turn the key.
    4. Pivot pins you can actually fit. A tangential pin can't be inserted from outside a ring of slots. 3 mm dowels drop into grooves from below; the retainer plate's fingers hold them up.
    5. Steel eye bolt, not a printed ring. An M5 eye bolt runs through the centre and clamps the retainer. A printed bail would snap along the layer lines.
    6. Key held by its ring hole. A pin through the fork and the key's hole replaces the spring clip, which would only fit one key shape.
