# NSO_Repair fixtures

Test meshes for the non-manifold repair module. Not shipped to Pages as product
files, and not loaded by the app.

Every `synth_*.stl` here is generated by `tools/nso_repair_make_fixtures.js`
from literal coordinates — nothing random — and committed so the regression
suite runs from a clean checkout with no generation step. To regenerate:

```sh
node tools/nso_repair_make_fixtures.js           # rewrite them
node tools/nso_repair_make_fixtures.js --check   # byte-diff against the tree
```

Six of the eight carry one named defect each on the same 20 mm origin-centred
cube (12 triangles, volume exactly 8000 mm^3), so the expected arithmetic is
checkable by hand: one bottom triangle of that cube spans a tetrahedron of
exactly 8000/12 = 666.667 mm^3 against the origin.

| file | tris | defect | after repair |
|---|---|---|---|
| `synth_hole.stl` | 11 | one triangle deleted, 3-edge boundary loop | 12 tris, volume 8000 |
| `synth_dup_exact.stl` | 13 | triangle 0 duplicated, same winding | 12 tris, volume 8000 |
| `synth_dup_reversed.stl` | 13 | triangle 0 duplicated, winding flipped | 12 tris, volume 8000 |
| `synth_flap.stl` | 13 | extra triangle hanging off edge F-G | 12 tris, volume 8000 |
| `synth_tjunction.stl` | 13 | vertex on the interior of edge F-G | 14 tris, volume 8000 |
| `synth_near_dup_vertex.stl` | 12 | every vertex slot jittered by +/-2e-5 mm | 12 tris, welds to 8 verts |

The last two exercise the gate rather than a repair. Both are watertight and
self-intersection-free to begin with; the only defect is one pinch vertex where
two sheets meet.

| file | tris | pinch angle | expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| `synth_pinch_2sheet_safe.stl` | 12 | 180 deg — two pyramids tip to tip | separates cleanly, gate passes |
| `synth_pinch_2sheet_tight.stl` | 20 | 0 deg — a spike standing coaxially in a pit | gate blocks, 0 -> 12 self-intersections, file unchanged |

`thingi10k/` holds the three real-mesh cases. They are not committed — see the
README in that directory.
